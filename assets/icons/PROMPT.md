Create one polished, bold, memorable illustrated app icon, full-bleed opaque square, 1024x1024 composition. Match the existing app palette: deep emerald felt green #124c39 background, navy-blue tarot card backs #193d50, warm antique gold #c8af77 and cream #eee4c9. Subject: a compact fan of three elegant tarot cards, the foremost card with a simple gold four-point star and crescent moon, fine diamond pattern hinted sparingly on the backs. Flat graphic illustration with restrained, tactile vintage printed-paper character, crisp broad shapes and strong contrast, readable at 48 pixels. Center all important symbols and all three cards inside the middle 68% of the square, leaving generous green breathing room on all sides so Android circular and rounded masks cannot cut the symbol. Background fills every edge, no pre-rounded outer corners. No words, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no phones, no app mockup. Deliver the icon artwork only.
